# 📱 Omnichannel Marketing Strategy – Samsung Galaxy Ring  
**Full Campaign Design | Politecnico di Milano – 2024/2025**

This project presents a complete **Omnichannel Marketing Campaign** designed for the launch of the **Samsung Galaxy Ring**, integrating **SWOT, PESTEL, segmentation, customer journey**, and a 24-week cross-channel media plan.

The campaign was developed as a full strategic marketing case study covering **brand building, customer engagement, KPI modelling, and ROI/ROMI forecasting**.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The objective was to design an omnichannel strategy that:

- Introduces **Galaxy Ring** as a premium wellness–tech product  
- Builds a **health-driven digital community** around Samsung ecosystem  
- Enhances **brand perception** with emotional & social engagement  
- Integrates all customer touchpoints: **paid + owned + earned media**  
- Optimizes the funnel using **AIDAR model**  
- Calculates **expected audience**, **marketing expenditure**, and **financial returns**

The final output includes: full report, slides, market research, cost modelling, and campaign previews.
